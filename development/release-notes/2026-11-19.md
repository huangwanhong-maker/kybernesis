# Release Notes: 19 November 2026

## 2. The Record of a Steering, and Practices Carried by a Lens

kybernesis now keeps the record of a steering and carries practices from one system to another ([decision 0004](../decisions/0004-the-record-and-transfer.md)).

- **The record.** It keeps every value of every block at every turn with how it came to be:
  - computed by a method;
  - observed (what the world does enters only by observation, never computed);
  - entered by a person;
  - amended, with the computed value kept beside it;
  - accepted or refused in a review.
- **Persons' entries are held to what can be held.** An entry must be of the block's type and within the block's bound: an estimate that excludes everything the evidence allows is refused. It counts only where the person's role is empowered; otherwise it is a proposal awaiting a review.
- **What changes, and what is kept.**
  - Files are named by their digests, and every act is sealed in archeion's journal.
  - When an input changes, what methods made from it is recomputed; a person's entry is reported stale, never replaced.
  - A branch re-reads a turn under another reading without touching the main line.
  - The record can be replayed, compared, searched for what rests on an entry, a file or a person, and given as W3C PROV.
- **Transfer.** A practice is carried by a lens, and three answers are given:
  - where the two systems' mechanisms agree, decided exactly by kategoria and measured within a tolerance;
  - which support factors that agreement needs;
  - whether "it will work here" stands over the readings of the target's terms.
- kybernesis now uses archeion. 137 checks in eight programs.

## 1. Founded: The Architecture of Steering as Data, Methods Admitted to Its Blocks, the Loop Run

kybernesis is founded: a library for steering a system known only through evidence, from what is observed, through what is concluded and decided, to what is done.

- **The architecture is data.** The loop from evidence to action is declared in a script ([architectures/standard.arch](../../architectures/standard.arch)), and anyone may revise it: a block, a law, a kind or a wire added, removed or changed.
  - A revision is admitted only when every check holds. Some are constraints on the declaration: every input fed once, every output used, nothing computed from the system except through what is observed. Others are categorical claims answered by kategoria.
  - A faulty revision is refused by the rule that names its fault, and what a revision changes is reported by name.
- **Any method can fill a block, once shown to fit.** An algorithm written for another purpose comes in through an adapter.
  - It is admitted when its ports are the block's and kategoria decides the block's laws on a finite instance: it answers on every input, it is deterministic where it must be, and an estimator never loses the true state.
  - A method that only claims a law is refused: the estimator that takes a reading at its word is lawful in form, unsound in fact, and refused.
- **Methods ready to use, formal and statistical:**
  - interpretation by declared readings, by construal and by precedents;
  - estimation by possibilities and by Bayes's rule;
  - evaluation of a probability under every admissible reading of its terms;
  - policy by a threshold or by solving the POMDP.

  An invented riverside example fills every block.
- **Two answers to every conclusion:** how firmly it stands over the readings of its terms, and how probable it is. A declared standard says how they combine, and a model's word never counts as the record's.
- **The loop runs** any admitted architecture, a revised one included, the same each time it is repeated.
- 86 checks; the schema's 42 statements are answered by kategoria; the infrastructure draws the architecture as a computational graph.
