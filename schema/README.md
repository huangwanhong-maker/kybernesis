# Schema

[framework.cat](framework.cat) states kybernesis's schema as categories, in kategoria's language:

- the kinds of fact, as a conservative extension of the law domain's, with the examples a domain may declare read as evaluations;
- one step of the loop, as a symmetric monoidal theory with models among functions and relations;
- the record, as a set-valued functor on a schema with nine rules.

The architecture module's `check_schema` answers its 42 statements by kategoria's session, and holds the standard architecture's kinds to the schema's. It began as the seed of the infrastructure's idea of 2026-11-18.
