# Architectures

Architecture scripts, read by `architecture/language.hpp` and admitted by `architecture/checks.hpp`.

| Script | Holds | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [standard.arch](standard.arch) | The standard architecture: the loop from evidence to action across the three registers. Three boundaries: the constitution, proposals, practice of other systems. Ten blocks: the system, collection, evidence, interpretation, estimation, evaluation, policy making, policy, decision, realisation | draft |

A revision is another script with a greater version. The checks hold revisions made in code: a block of transfer added (admitted), interpretation removed without rewiring (refused).
